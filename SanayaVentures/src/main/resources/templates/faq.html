General Questions

What is SA Sanaya Ventures?
SA Sanaya Ventures is an online product discovery platform that showcases carefully selected products across categories such as electronics, accessories, watches, home decor and kitchen essentials.
How does SA Sanaya Ventures work?
You can explore products on our website, compare the information available and click a shopping link to visit the relevant marketplace for your purchase.
Do I need an account to browse products?
No. You can browse our website and explore listed products without creating an account.

Products & Shopping

How can I purchase a product?
Open the product you like and select the available marketplace button, such as Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho or Myntra. You will be redirected to that marketplace to complete your purchase.
Are all products sold directly by SA Sanaya Ventures?
No. SA Sanaya Ventures is a product discovery and affiliate platform. Purchases are completed through the respective third-party marketplaces.
Are the prices displayed on the website final?
Displayed prices are provided for reference and may change. Please check the final price on the marketplace before placing your order.

Orders, Delivery & Returns

Who handles payment and delivery?
Payments, order processing, shipping and delivery are handled by the marketplace where you place your order.
How can I track my order?
You can track your order through the order history or tracking section of the marketplace where you completed your purchase.
What if I want to return or exchange a product?
Returns, exchanges and refunds are subject to the respective marketplace's policies. Please contact that marketplace for assistance with your order.

Affiliate Links & Support

What are affiliate links?
Some links on our website are affiliate links. If you make a qualifying purchase through these links, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.
Does using an affiliate link increase the product price?
No. Using an affiliate link does not add an extra charge to your purchase. The final price is determined by the marketplace.
How can I contact SA Sanaya Ventures?
You can visit our Contact Us page to find the available ways to get in touch with us.
